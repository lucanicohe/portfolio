The skills on the right are only a selection. I also work across a much wider range of technical and creative areas, depending on what a project needs.

  • Unity development beyond programming, with a focus on finishing and polishing games, shaders, Shader Graph, UI, interaction, and overall presentation.
  • Procedural 3D modeling and environment creation with World Machine.
  • AI-assisted creative workflows, including Stable Diffusion for image generation and experimentation.
  • Visual design and UI work, combining technical implementation with a strong focus on presentation.
  • 3D production across Blender, Cinema 4D, Blockbench, and other tools.
  • WebGL, most of my solo projects are playable in browser using WebGL.
  • Digital drawing and painting with Clip Studio Paint, ranging from realistic illustration to anime-style artwork.
  • GitHub, this portfolio exists here as well as most of my games.
  • HTML, I wrote this portfolio in HTML without using a service.
  • Development across both macOS and Windows environments.

Selected work

Artwork Restoration

A collaborative interactive artwork restoration project developed with illustrator @chip_illustrator . Her illustration work was transformed into an interactive digital experience through programming, animation and technical implementation.

Lost in the Cold

A procedural exploration game that constructs a new maze layout on every launch. The player must navigate an ever-changing frozen maze, demonstrating algorithmic generation and replayable level structure.

Play

Earth is a Toy

A physics-based alien catching game where animated aliens react to the player's actions. I used Inverse Kinematics (IK) to create procedural character animations, allowing the aliens' bodies and limbs to naturally react to the movement and direction of the gameplay.

Play

Only Bad Guys on the Train

A first-person combat experience set entirely within a moving train. I modeled everything myself, including all 3D assets, 2D elements and textures. The complete visual content is 100% my own creation, from the models and environments to the textures and artwork.

Play

Olympus Dodge

A first-person fantasy dodgeball game inspired by the feeling of exploring a world like Skyrim. I created the environment background in World Machine and used it to build a large fantasy landscape around the gameplay.

Play

Frog Kamizumo

A 3D reinterpretation of traditional Japanese Kamizumo, transforming the classic paper sumo game into a physical 3D experience. The main technical challenge was creating convincing physics that make the sumo characters react naturally to movement, collisions and impacts.

Play

Underwater Pinball

An old-school pinball game set underwater, developed entirely in Unity. I modeled all of the game's 3D assets myself and programmed the complete gameplay and pinball mechanics.

Play

Bongo 3D

A 3D interactive reinterpretation of the Bongo Cat internet phenomenon, combining character interaction, animation and responsive gameplay into a browser-based experience.

Play

Bow & Bones

A stylized 2D archery experience combining hand-drawn visuals with projectile mechanics, enemy encounters and responsive combat.

Play

Zero Gravity Bowling

A physics-driven bowling experiment that removes conventional gravity, requiring the player to read momentum, trajectories and spatial movement in zero gravity.

Play

The Floor Is Lava

A movement-focused traversal experience set inside a flooded classroom, turning the environment itself into the central obstacle and movement system.

Play

Tron Rhythm

A rhythm-based dodge game where obstacles are spawned in sync with the rhythm of the music. The player must follow the beat while avoiding incoming cubes, combining simple movement with music-driven gameplay.

Play

The Universe

A fully hand-drawn animation piece rather than a playable game, imagining a simplified and artistic version of the universe. Meditation on scale and existence rather than something with rules to learn.
