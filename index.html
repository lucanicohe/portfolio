Selected work

Team project — Unity — School award winner

Guilty Lane

A first-person train game built around a perspective and a UI concept I pitched myself — and it went on to win an award at school.

I pushed for the first-person perspective early on and designed the interface to live inside the world itself, projected onto in-game screens, rather than sitting on top of it as a flat overlay — a choice that became one of the game's most memorable touches. On the technical side, I programmed the procedural system that spawns the train tracks and built the train itself, and I modeled, textured, and art-directed every 3D asset in the game from scratch.

Kumo vs. Mahou

An open-world fantasy game I led from the very first idea through to a finished, playable build.

Kumo vs. Mahou started as my own concept — an open world where players hunt down spiders using magic — and I stayed in the driver's seat for the whole production. As project lead I set the direction and kept the team organized and on schedule, while personally writing the core gameplay scripts and modeling and texturing the 3D assets that shaped the world's look and feel.
